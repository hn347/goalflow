# GoalFlow Pitch

GoalFlow is a Bob-native integration that adds `/goal`, turning Bob into a goal-driven engineering loop. It uses Bob IDE subagents for isolated discovery and independent judging, Bob custom modes for orchestrated implementation, MCP for live documentation and IBM Carbon design guidance, and artifact-based verification to prevent scope drift.

## Why it matters

Enterprise teams do not only need generated code. They need a contract, evidence, and a judge. GoalFlow gives Bob those governance primitives while staying inside Bob's native surfaces: slash commands, custom modes, mode rules, subagents, MCP, and QA workflows.

## Demo tagline

GoalFlow does not just make Bob code. It gives Bob a contract, evidence, and a judge.
